# Initiative Brief: Daily Hydration Tracker

## What

A mobile-first web app that helps people track daily water intake, set personalized hydration goals, and build consistent habits through streaks and gentle reminders.

## Problem

Chronic dehydration is one of the most overlooked health crises. It's linked to kidney disease, cognitive decline, cardiovascular strain, and increased risk of UTIs — conditions that cost healthcare systems billions annually. Studies show that even mild dehydration (1-2% body water loss) reduces concentration by up to 25% and increases fatigue, headaches, and anxiety.

The irony: the fix is free and available everywhere. But most people still don't drink enough water. Not because they don't know — because they don't have a routine.

Existing solutions fail at this. They're either buried inside complex health super-apps where hydration is an afterthought, or they're over-gamified toys that feel fun for 3 days and get abandoned. None of them are designed around the real challenge: **turning hydration into an automatic, daily routine**.

## Our Moat

**Hydration as a routine.** Not a feature, not a game — a behavior change. If we can help people build and sustain the habit of consistent daily hydration, the downstream health impact is massive: fewer kidney problems, sharper cognition, better cardiovascular health, reduced healthcare costs. One glass of water at a time, repeated daily, at scale.

We're not building a water counter. We're building the simplest path to a healthier default.

## Who

- **Primary:** Health-conscious professionals (25-45) who spend most of their day at a desk and forget to hydrate
- **Secondary:** People starting a wellness routine who want one small, achievable daily habit

## Core Experience

1. **Quick Log** — Tap to log water intake (preset amounts: 200ml, 300ml, 500ml, custom)
2. **Daily Goal** — Personalized target based on weight/activity level (default: 2L)
3. **Progress View** — Visual progress bar/ring showing how close to the daily goal
4. **Streaks** — Track consecutive days of hitting the goal to reinforce habit
5. **History** — Simple weekly/monthly view of hydration patterns
6. **Reminders** — Optional push notifications at configurable intervals

## What Success Looks Like

- User can log intake in under 3 seconds (zero friction)
- 60% of active users hit their daily goal at least 4x/week
- 7-day retention > 40%

## Constraints

- Mobile-first responsive web (no native app for MVP)
- No account required for basic tracking (local storage), optional sign-up for sync/history
- Lightweight — should feel instant, no loading states
- Accessible: WCAG AA compliant

## Out of Scope (MVP)

- Integration with wearables or health platforms
- Social features (sharing, leaderboards)
- Tracking other beverages or nutrients
- Premium/paid features
